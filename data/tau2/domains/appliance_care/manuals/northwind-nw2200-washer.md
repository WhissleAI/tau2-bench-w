# Northwind NW-2200 Front-Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual

**Models covered:** NW-2200, NW-2200-W, NW-2200-S
**Not covered:** NW-2200X (see the NW-2200X manual — different filter assembly),
NW-2400 (different cabinet, different filter location)

---

## 1. Control panel

Left to right: **Power** button, **Cycle** dial (Cotton, Synthetics, Delicates,
Quick 30, Drum Clean, Rinse & Spin, Drain Only), **Temp** button, **Spin** button,
**Delay** button, a two-character **display**, and the **Start/Pause** button.

The display shows the remaining time during a cycle, and an error code if the
machine stops with a fault. A padlock symbol next to the display means the door is
locked; it clears about two minutes after the drum stops.

---

## 2. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `E11` | Not filling with water | Check both inlet taps are open and the inlet hose is not kinked. Clean the inlet screen (§5.3). |
| `E24` | **Drain fault** — the machine cannot pump water out | Clean the drain filter (§5.1) and check the drain hose (§5.2). |
| `E45` | Door lock fault — the door is not latched | Open and firmly re-close the door. Check nothing is trapped in the seal. |
| `E52` | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry and restart. Avoid washing a single heavy item alone. |
| `F03` | Motor thermal cut-out | **Service required.** Stop using the machine and arrange a visit. Do not restart repeatedly. |
| `F07` | Water detected in the base tray | **Service required.** Stop using the machine, unplug it, and arrange a visit. |

> **WARNING:** `F03` and `F07` are not customer-serviceable. Repeatedly restarting
> a machine showing either code can cause overheating or an electrical fault.

---

## 3. Approved reset

The NW-2200 supports one customer reset. Use it after clearing a fault, or if the
machine is unresponsive but shows no error code:

1. Press **Power** to switch the machine off.
2. Hold **Start/Pause** for **5 seconds** until the display blinks twice.
3. Press **Power** to switch it back on and select a cycle.

> **WARNING:** Do not repeat the reset if the household circuit breaker or RCD
> trips when the machine is switched on or started. A tripping breaker indicates
> an electrical fault. Stop using the machine and arrange service.

Perform the reset at most twice. If the same fault returns, the machine needs
service.

- Inlet-hose screen (part NW-S10)

## 5. Maintenance and troubleshooting procedures

### 5.1 Cleaning the drain filter

The drain filter is behind the **kick-panel flap at the bottom front right** of
the cabinet. It catches coins, buttons, and lint, and is the most common cause of
a machine that will not drain.

> **WARNING:** Switch the machine off and unplug it before opening the filter
> flap. Water in the drum may be hot. Never open the filter while a cycle is
> running.

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Open the kick-panel flap at the bottom front right by pressing its left edge.
3. Place a shallow tray and a towel under the flap. **Up to 2 litres of water may
   drain out.**
4. Pull out the short black drain-down hose, remove its cap, and let the water
   run into the tray. Refit the cap when the flow stops.
5. Turn the round filter cartridge **anti-clockwise** and withdraw it.
6. Remove any debris. Rinse the mesh under a tap. Check the impeller opening
   behind the filter is clear of objects — **look only, do not reach in.**
7. Refit the cartridge and turn it **clockwise** until it is firm and the arrow
   points up. A filter that is not fully seated will leak.
8. Close the flap, plug the machine in, and run **Rinse & Spin** to confirm it
   drains.

If the machine still does not drain after cleaning the filter, check the drain
hose (§5.2). If both are clear, the drain pump needs service.

### 5.2 Checking the drain hose

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Check the hose behind the machine is not kinked, crushed against the wall, or
   trapped under a foot.
3. Check the hose end is not pushed more than **10 cm** into a standpipe. Pushing
   it further can siphon or block the flow.
4. If the hose runs to a sink trap, check that the trap connection is clear.

> **WARNING:** Do not detach the drain hose from the machine's own outlet. That
> connection is inside the cabinet and is not customer-serviceable.

### 5.3 Cleaning the inlet screen

1. Turn off the water taps. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Unscrew the inlet hose from the **tap end** only.
3. Lift out the small mesh screen with pliers, rinse it, and refit it.
4. Reconnect the hose hand-tight, open the tap, and check for drips.

### 5.4 Drum Clean cycle

Run **Drum Clean** monthly, with an empty drum and no detergent, to control odour
and residue. It takes about 90 minutes and cannot be paused.

### 5.5 Troubleshooting table

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Will not drain; water left in the drum | Blocked drain filter | Clean the drain filter (§5.1) |
| Will not drain; filter clean | Kinked or badly fitted drain hose | Check the drain hose (§5.2) |
| Will not drain; filter and hose clear | Drain pump fault | Arrange service |
| Will not start; display shows `E45` | Door not fully latched | Re-close the door firmly |
| Will not start; no display at all | No power at the socket | Check the socket with another appliance |
| Not filling | Tap closed or inlet screen blocked | §5.3 |
| Very noisy on spin | Unbalanced load, or transit bolts still fitted | Redistribute; confirm transit bolts were removed at installation |
| Grinding or metal-on-metal noise | Foreign object or bearing fault | **Stop using the machine. Arrange service.** |
| Leaking from the bottom front | Filter not fully seated | Re-seat the filter (§5.1 step 7) |
| Breaker trips when switched on | Electrical fault | **Stop using the machine. Arrange service.** |
| Burning or electrical smell | Electrical fault | **Stop immediately, unplug, arrange service.** |

## 6. Safety — service required

> **WARNING:** Switch the machine off, unplug it if the plug is dry and
> reachable, and arrange an authorized service visit if you notice any of the
> following. Do not continue to use the machine.
>
> - Smoke, sparks, scorching, or a burning or electrical smell
> - A cabinet, door glass, or plug that is hot to the touch
> - A grinding or screeching noise under load
> - The circuit breaker or RCD tripping
> - Water pooling near the plug, socket, or extension lead

Do not remove the top, rear, or front panel. Do not open the terminal block. Do
not defeat the door interlock. There are no user-serviceable parts inside the
cabinet.
