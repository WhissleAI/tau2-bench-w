# Bosch 300 Series WAT28400UC Front-Load Washer — benchmark extract

**Model covered by this document:** `WAT28400UC` (the manual names this model only)
**Document code:** `9001002399_H`
**Do not use for:** `WAT28401UC` or `WAT28402UC` — those are separate models with their own manuals.

---

## 1. Display and error codes

Faults appear on the display as `E:` followed by two digits. This model's manual does **not** document `E:23`; that code appears only in the WAT28402UC manual.

| Code | Manual text | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `E:18` | Pump is blocked (obstructed). Also shown when too much detergent or non-HE detergent was used. | Clean the washer drain hose and/or the drain pipe. Use less detergent; only use HE detergents. |
| `E:32` | **Not a fault** - the unbalanced load detection system has interrupted the spin cycle because the laundry is unevenly distributed. | Distribute small and large items of laundry evenly in the drum. Restart if needed. |
| `E:93` | Hot water tap not turned on, or the supply hose for hot water is kinked or pinched closed. | Turn on the hot water tap and straighten the hose. |


---

## 2. Cleaning the drain hose and drain pipe (drain filter access)

The manual's customer action for `E:18` is to clean the washer **drain hose** and/or
the **drain pipe**. That is the only drain-path cleaning this model documents for a
customer; there is no separate pull-out drain filter cartridge procedure.

---

## 3. Customer-serviceable items

The manual documents customer cleaning of the **drain hose** and **drain pipe**
(reached for `E:18`), the **detergent dispenser drawer**, and the **levelling feet**.

Anything beyond those is after-sales service work. The manual gives no customer
procedure for opening the pump housing, the base tub, or any electrical part.

---

## 4. Reset

"Turn off machine, wait 5 seconds and turn on again. If the display shows again,
call customer service." This is the only reset the manual documents, and it is the
recommended first step for any code not listed above.

---

## 5. Safety — stop use

The manual instructs the customer to disconnect the appliance and contact service
where a fault indicates water escaping the drum or any sign of electrical damage.
An agent must not walk a customer through continued operation in those conditions.

Signals that require stopping immediately, regardless of any code shown: smoke, a
burning or chemical smell, sparks, a hot cabinet, or water pooling under the machine.
