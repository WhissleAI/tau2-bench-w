# Northwind NW-2200X Front-Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual

**Models covered:** NW-2200X only
**Not covered:** NW-2200 / NW-2200-W / NW-2200-S / NW-2400

> **MODEL-SPECIFIC EXCEPTION.** The NW-2200X shares the NW-2200 cabinet and
> control panel, but its drain path is different: it has **two** filters, and the
> secondary lint filter must be removed **before** the main cartridge or the
> cartridge will not release. The main filter is part **NW-F22X**, which is not
> interchangeable with the NW-F22 fitted to the NW-2200.
>
> Telling an NW-2200X owner to follow the NW-2200 filter procedure will leave the
> secondary filter blocked and the machine still will not drain.

---

## 1. Control panel

Identical to the NW-2200: **Power**, **Cycle** dial (Cotton, Synthetics,
Delicates, Quick 30, Drum Clean, Rinse & Spin, Drain Only), **Temp**, **Spin**,
**Delay**, two-character **display**, **Start/Pause**.

The `X` suffix appears on the rating label only. The two models cannot be told
apart from the front of the machine.

---

## 2. Error codes

Same code set as the NW-2200:

| Code | Meaning | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `E11` | Not filling with water | Check the taps; clean the inlet screen. |
| `E24` | **Drain fault** | Clean **both** filters (§5.1) and check the drain hose. |
| `E45` | Door lock fault | Re-close the door firmly. |
| `E52` | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry. |
| `F03` | Motor thermal cut-out | **Service required.** Stop using the machine. |
| `F07` | Water in the base tray | **Service required.** Unplug and arrange a visit. |

---

## 3. Approved reset

As on the NW-2200: switch off, hold **Start/Pause** for 5 seconds until the
display blinks twice, switch back on. Do not repeat if the household breaker
trips — that indicates an electrical fault and needs service.

## 4. Customer-serviceable parts

- Main drain filter (part **NW-F22X**)
- Secondary lint filter (part **NW-L05**)
- Inlet-hose screen (part NW-S10)

## 5. Maintenance and troubleshooting procedures

### 5.1 Cleaning both drain filters

Both filters sit behind the **kick-panel flap at the bottom front right**.

> **WARNING:** Switch off and unplug the machine before opening the flap. Water in
> the drum may be hot. Never open the flap during a cycle.

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Open the kick-panel flap at the bottom front right.
3. Place a tray and towels under the flap. Up to 2 litres of water may drain out.
4. Pull out the drain-down hose, uncap it, drain into the tray, and re-cap it.
5. **First**, slide out the flat **secondary lint filter** from the slot above the
   cartridge. Rinse it and set it aside.
6. **Then** turn the main cartridge anti-clockwise and withdraw it. It will not
   release if the lint filter is still fitted — do not force it.
7. Clear debris from both, rinse under a tap, and check the opening behind the
   cartridge is clear. **Look only, do not reach in.**
8. Refit the main cartridge clockwise until firm, then slide the lint filter back
   into its slot until it clicks.
9. Close the flap, plug in, and run **Rinse & Spin** to confirm it drains.

### 5.2 Checking the drain hose

As on the NW-2200: check for kinks and crushing, and that the hose end sits no
more than 10 cm inside a standpipe. Do not detach the hose at the machine end.

### 5.3 Troubleshooting table

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Will not drain; `E24` | One or both filters blocked | §5.1 — the lint filter first |
| Main cartridge will not turn | Secondary lint filter still fitted | Remove the lint filter first (§5.1 step 5) |
| Still will not drain; both clear | Kinked hose, then pump fault | §5.2, then arrange service |
| Grinding noise | Foreign object or bearing fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Breaker trips | Electrical fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Burning smell | Electrical fault | **Stop immediately, unplug, arrange service.** |

## 6. Safety — service required

> **WARNING:** Stop using the machine and arrange an authorized visit for smoke,
> sparks, a burning or electrical smell, a hot cabinet or plug, a grinding noise
> under load, a tripping breaker, or water near the socket. No panel is to be
> removed. The door interlock must never be defeated.
