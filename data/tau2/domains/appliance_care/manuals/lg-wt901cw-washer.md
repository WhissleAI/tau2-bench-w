# LG WT901CW Top-Load Washer — benchmark extract

**Model covered by this document:** `WT901CW` (the manual names this exact model)
**Document code:** `MFL68485601_06`
**Form factor:** top-load. Procedures here do **not** transfer to a front-load
machine, and front-load procedures do not transfer here.

---

## 1. Display messages

LG uses **letter codes**, not the `E:nn` numeric form Bosch uses. A customer reading
out something like `E:18` or `E:23` is not quoting a code from this manual, and the
agent must not map it onto an LG meaning.

| Code | Manual text | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `IE` | May blink on the display when the water pressure is weak or an inlet filter is clogged with dirt. | Clean the inlet filter. |
| `CL` | **Not a fault** — Child Lock is active. Displays alternately with the estimated time remaining. | Press and hold the **SOIL LEVEL** button for five seconds to deactivate Child Lock. |

> Once the controls are locked, they must be unlocked with the same button before
> any other control responds. A customer reporting "none of the buttons work" with
> `CL` showing needs the lock cleared, not a service visit.

---

## 2. Customer-serviceable items

The manual documents customer cleaning of the **lint filters** and the **water inlet
filters**, plus the fabric softener cup and levelling feet.

**Lint filters:** "Clean the lint filters at least every 2-3 loads." The manual
describes lifting a triangular tab to open the filter.

**Inlet filters:** cleaned in response to `IE` (weak water pressure or a clogged
filter).

---

## 3. Reset

This model does **not** document a customer reset sequence of the kind the Bosch
models publish. Do not tell a customer to perform one.

---

## 4. Storage

"If the washer will not be used for an extended period of time" the manual gives a
storage procedure. This is the documented answer to a long-absence question — not a
drain-down or winterisation procedure invented on the spot.

---

## 5. Safety — stop use

Stop use immediately and arrange service, regardless of any code displayed, on:
smoke, a burning or chemical smell, sparks, a hot cabinet, or water pooling under
the machine. Do not continue running a cycle to "see if it clears".
