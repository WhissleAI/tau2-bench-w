# Northwind NW-2400 Front-Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual

**Models covered:** NW-2400, NW-2400-W
**Not covered:** NW-2200 / NW-2200X (different cabinet — the filter is on the
opposite side and the error codes are not the same)

> **IMPORTANT — do not apply NW-2200 instructions to this machine.** On the
> NW-2400 the drain filter is at the bottom front **left**, and error code `E24`
> means a **door lock** fault, not a drain fault. The drain fault code on this
> model is `E31`.

---

## 1. Control panel

A single **Power** button, a **Cycle** dial (Cotton, Eco Cotton, Synthetics,
Wool, Quick 40, Drum Clean, Spin Only), **Temp**, **Spin**, **Delay**, a
three-character **display**, and **Start/Pause**.

---

## 2. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `E12` | Not filling with water | Check the taps are open; clean the inlet screen (§5.3). |
| `E24` | **Door lock fault** — the latch has not engaged | Open the door and re-close it firmly. Check the seal for trapped fabric. |
| `E31` | **Drain fault** — water cannot be pumped out | Clean the drain filter (§5.1), then check the drain hose (§5.2). |
| `E55` | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry and restart. |
| `F09` | Motor or control fault | **Service required.** Stop using the machine. |
| `F14` | Water in the base tray | **Service required.** Stop using the machine, unplug it. |

---

## 3. Reset

**The NW-2400 has no customer reset procedure.** If the machine is unresponsive,
switch it off at the wall for 60 seconds and switch it back on. If the fault
returns, arrange service. Do not attempt a button-combination reset — the button
sequences used on other Northwind models do not apply to this cabinet.

---

## 4. Customer-serviceable parts

- Drain filter (part **NW-F24** — not interchangeable with NW-F22)
- Inlet-hose screen (part NW-S10)
- Detergent drawer

## 5. Maintenance and troubleshooting procedures

### 5.1 Cleaning the drain filter

On this model the drain filter is behind the **hinged cover at the bottom front
left** of the cabinet. There is no drain-down hose on the NW-2400; the filter
housing itself holds the residual water.

> **WARNING:** Switch off and unplug the machine first. Expect up to 1.5 litres of
> water, which may be hot, to release when the cartridge is withdrawn. Have a tray
> and towels ready before you start.

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Open the hinged cover at the bottom front left with a coin in the slot.
3. Place a shallow tray and towels under the opening.
4. Turn the filter cartridge **anti-clockwise** and withdraw it **slowly** so the
   water releases gradually.
5. Clear debris, rinse the mesh, and check the opening behind it is clear —
   **look only, do not reach in.**
6. Refit the cartridge and turn it **clockwise** until the arrow aligns with the
   mark on the housing.
7. Close the cover, plug in, and run **Spin Only** to confirm it drains.

### 5.2 Checking the drain hose

As on other models: check for kinks, crushing behind the machine, and that the
hose end sits no more than 10 cm inside a standpipe. Do not detach the hose from
the machine's own outlet.

### 5.3 Cleaning the inlet screen

Turn off the taps, unplug, unscrew the hose at the **tap end** only, rinse the
mesh screen, and refit hand-tight.

### 5.4 Troubleshooting table

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Water left in the drum; `E31` | Blocked drain filter | §5.1 |
| Will not start; `E24` | Door not latched | Re-close the door firmly |
| Not filling; `E12` | Tap closed or blocked inlet screen | §5.3 |
| Noisy spin | Unbalanced load | Redistribute |
| Grinding noise | Foreign object or bearing fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Breaker trips | Electrical fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Burning smell | Electrical fault | **Stop immediately, unplug, arrange service.** |

## 6. Safety — service required

> **WARNING:** Stop using the machine and arrange an authorized service visit for
> smoke, sparks, a burning or electrical smell, a hot cabinet or plug, a grinding
> noise under load, a tripping breaker, or water near the socket. Do not remove
> any panel. Do not defeat the door interlock.
