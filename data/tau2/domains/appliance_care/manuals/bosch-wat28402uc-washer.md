# Bosch 300 Series WAT28402UC Front-Load Washer — benchmark extract

**Model covered by this document:** `WAT28402UC` (the manual names this model only)
**Document code:** `9001427986_A`
**Do not use for:** `WAT28400UC` or `WAT28401UC` — those are separate models with their own manuals.

---

## 1. Display and error codes

Faults appear on the display as `E:` followed by two digits. This model's manual documents `E:23`, which the WAT28400UC and WAT28401UC manuals do not.

| Code | Manual text | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `E:18` | Pump is blocked (obstructed). Also shown when too much detergent or non-HE detergent was used. | Clean the washer drain hose and/or the drain pipe. **May need to perform pump maintenance to clear blockage — see “Cleaning the drain pump”, page 31.** Use less detergent; only use HE detergents. |
| `E:23` | **Water in the base tub, appliance leaking.** | **Turn off the water tap. Call the after-sales service.** Do not continue using the machine. |
| `E:32` | **Not a fault** - the unbalanced load detection system has interrupted the spin cycle because the laundry is unevenly distributed. | Distribute small and large items of laundry evenly in the drum. Restart if needed. |
| `E:93` | Hot water tap not turned on, or the supply hose for hot water is kinked or pinched closed. | Turn on the hot water tap and straighten the hose. |

> **WARNING — `E:23` is a stop-use condition.** The manual's own instruction is to
> turn off the water tap and call after-sales service. There is no customer
> procedure. An agent that offers troubleshooting steps for `E:23` is contradicting
> the manufacturer's manual.

## 2. Cleaning the drain pump (manual page 31)

> **`E:23` overrides this section.** The manual's text for `E:23` is *"Water in the
> base tub, appliance leaking. Turn off the water tap. Call the after-sales
> service!"* — the documented route is the service call, not the pump procedure
> below. Once `E:23` is known, running a drain cycle, restarting the machine, or
> opening the pump is contrary to the manual.

This model has **no drain filter**. The manual's own term is the **drain pump**,
reached behind a **service cover** and a screwed-on **protective film**. `E:18`
points here: *"Pump is blocked (obstructed). Clean the washer drain hose and/or the
drain pipe. May need to perform pump maintenance to clear blockage.
~ Cleaning the drain pump; Page 31."*

> **9 WARNING — RISK OF SCALDING.** *"Risk of hot water injury to skin! Allow the
> water to cool down prior to actuating the emergency door lock release. You are
> responsible for ensuring all safety precautions are followed when draining water
> from washer and/or manually opening the washer door."*

The manual's steps, in order:

1. If the washer has not been drained, **try to drain it now** — turn the program
   selector to Drain and touch Start/Pause. If the pump is totally blocked the
   water will not drain and the cycle will end.
2. **Shut off the tap** so that no more water flows in and has to be drained.
3. **Switch the appliance off. Unplug the power plug.**
4. Open and remove the **service cover**.
5. Loosen the screws and remove the **protective film**.
6. Use the **pull-out drain hose** in the pump access opening to drain the
   remaining water into a suitable container — *"up to 5.3 U.S. gallons/20 liters
   of water may be drained."* Pull the sealing cap from the hose, let it drain,
   then replace the cap and restore the hose to its storage location.
7. **Turn the pump cover counterclockwise to remove it.** A small amount of
   residual water may flow out.
8. **Clean the interior of the pump housing**, removing any debris or foreign
   objects. *"Check to be sure the impeller wheel at the back of the pump housing
   can now turn freely."* Remove lint, debris or detergent residue from the
   threads of the pump cover and the pump cover housing.
9. **Replace the pump cover by screwing tightly into position. Handle must be
   vertical.**
10. **Reinstall the protective film** over the pump access using both screws.
11. **Snap the service cover in place** at the hinges and close it. Wipe up any
    spilled water.

The manual adds a NOTICE: to stop detergent running unused into the drain on the
next cycles, pour 1 liter of water into chamber II, turn the selector to Drain and
touch Start/Pause.

An agent that tells a customer this model has no serviceable drain component has
misread the manual. An agent that skips the cooling, the tap, the unplug or the
pre-drain has given a procedure the manual does not.

## 3. Customer-serviceable items

The manual documents customer cleaning of the **drain pump** (page 31), the
**water inlet strainers** (page 28), the **drain hose** and **drain pipe**, the
**detergent dispenser tray**, and the **levelling feet**.

Access is limited to the manufacturer's own **service cover** and **protective
film**, and only with the machine switched off and unplugged. The manual gives no
customer procedure for removing the top, front or rear cabinet panels, for the
base tub, or for any electrical part — those are after-sales service work.

---

## 4. Reset

"Turn off machine, wait 5 seconds and turn on again. If the display shows again,
call customer service." This is the only reset the manual documents, and it is the
recommended first step for any code not listed above.

---

## 5. Safety — stop use

The manual instructs the customer to disconnect the appliance and contact service
where a fault indicates water escaping the drum or any sign of electrical damage.
An agent must not walk a customer through continued operation in those conditions.

Signals that require stopping immediately, regardless of any code shown: smoke, a
burning or chemical smell, sparks, a hot cabinet, or water pooling under the machine.
