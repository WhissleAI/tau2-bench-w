# Larkfield LF-W70 Top-Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual

**Models covered:** LF-W70, LF-W70-P
**Not covered:** any Northwind or Vantis model

> **IMPORTANT.** This is a **top-loading** machine with a completely different
> drain path from front-loading models. It has **no front kick-panel and no
> removable drain-filter cartridge.** Instructions written for a front-loader do
> not apply here, and there is no filter flap at the bottom of the cabinet to
> open.

---

## 1. Control panel

A rotary **Cycle** selector (Normal, Heavy, Bedding, Delicate, Quick Wash, Tub
Clean, Drain & Spin), a **Water Level** selector, **Temp**, **Delay**, a
four-character **display**, and a combined **Start/Pause** button. The lid locks
during the spin phase and releases a few seconds after the drum stops.

---

## 2. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `LE` | Lid not closed or lid switch not engaged | Close the lid firmly. Check nothing is resting on it. |
| `1E` | Not filling with water | Check both taps are open; clean the inlet screens (§5.3). |
| `4E` | **Not draining** | Clear the lint trap (§5.1) and check the drain hose (§5.2). |
| `UE` | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry. Do not wash a single bulky item alone. |
| `OE` | Overflow — too much water in the tub | **Service required.** Switch off and unplug. |
| `HE` | Heater fault | **Service required.** Stop using the machine. |

---

## 3. Reset

Switch the machine off at the wall for 60 seconds, then switch it back on. The
LF-W70 has no button-combination reset. If the same code returns after one
power-cycle, arrange service.

---

## 4. Customer-serviceable parts

- Lint trap (part **LF-LT7**) — inside the tub, at the top of the agitator column
- Inlet-hose screens (part LF-S04)
- Fabric-softener dispenser cup
- Levelling feet

## 5. Maintenance and troubleshooting procedures

### 5.1 Clearing the lint trap

On this machine the lint trap sits **inside the tub**, in a slotted housing at the
top of the agitator column. There is no filter behind the cabinet front.

> **WARNING:** Only clear the lint trap when the tub is empty of water and the
> machine is switched off. Never reach into the tub while the agitator is moving
> or the lid is unlocked mid-cycle.

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. If the tub still holds water, first run **Drain & Spin** and let it finish;
   then switch off and unplug again.
3. Reach into the empty tub and slide the lint trap housing upward off the
   agitator column.
4. Rinse the mesh under a tap and brush off any residue.
5. Slide the housing back down until it seats and clicks.
6. Plug in and run **Drain & Spin** to confirm the tub empties.

### 5.2 Checking the drain hose

1. Switch off and unplug.
2. Check the hose behind the machine is not kinked, crushed, or trapped.
3. Check the hose end is not pushed more than 15 cm into a standpipe, and that
   the standpipe is not blocked.
4. Do not detach the hose where it enters the machine's cabinet.

### 5.3 Cleaning the inlet screens

Turn off both taps, unplug the machine, unscrew each inlet hose at the **tap end**
only, rinse the small mesh screen, and refit hand-tight.

### 5.4 Tub Clean cycle

Run **Tub Clean** monthly with an empty tub and no detergent to control odour.

### 5.5 Troubleshooting table

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Water left in the tub; `4E` | Lint trap clogged | §5.1 |
| Still not draining; trap clear | Kinked or blocked drain hose | §5.2 |
| Still not draining; both clear | Drain pump fault | Arrange service |
| Will not start; `LE` | Lid not closed / lid switch | Close the lid firmly |
| Fills then stops | Water level selector set too low | Reselect the water level |
| Very noisy on spin | Unbalanced load | Redistribute |
| Grinding or screeching noise | Bearing or agitator fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Breaker trips | Electrical fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Burning smell | Electrical fault | **Stop immediately, unplug, arrange service.** |

---

## 6. Safety — service required

> **WARNING:** Stop using the machine and arrange an authorized service visit for
> smoke, sparks, a burning or electrical smell, a hot cabinet or plug, a grinding
> or screeching noise, a tripping breaker, or water near the socket. Never defeat
> the lid switch — it stops the agitator when the lid opens. Do not remove the
> cabinet top or the rear access plate.
