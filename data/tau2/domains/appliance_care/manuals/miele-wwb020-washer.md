# Miele WWB 020 Front-Load Washer — benchmark extract

**Model covered by this document:** `WWB 020` (the manual names this exact model)
**Document code:** `M.-Nr. 10 980 030`

---

## 1. How this model reports faults — no error codes

This is the important difference from the Bosch and LG models in this benchmark.

**The WWB 020 does not publish numeric or letter error codes.** Faults are signalled
by **indicator lights on the control field** and by symbols on the time display. The
manual's "Problem solving guide" is organised by *symptom*, not by code:

- A wash program cannot be started
- The program is canceled and a fault indicator light lights up on the control field
- During the program, a symbol appears on the time display
- At the end of the program, an indicator light lights up on the control field
- General problems with the washer
- Poor wash results
- The drum door cannot be opened
- Opening the door in the event of a blocked drain outlet and/or power outage

> A customer who reports "it's showing E:23" or "it says IE" on a WWB 020 has either
> misread the display or is describing a different appliance. There is **no** code
> table for this model to look it up in. The agent must resolve the discrepancy —
> confirm the model, or ask what the control field actually shows — and must not
> invent a meaning or borrow one from another manufacturer's manual.

---

## 2. Customer-serviceable items

The manual documents cleaning the **water inlet filter**, the detergent drawer, and
levelling the machine.

**There is no customer drain-filter cleaning procedure of the Bosch or LG kind.**
The manual covers *"Opening the door in the event of a blocked drain outlet and/or
power outage"* — that is a door-release procedure so laundry can be removed, **not**
a drain-cleaning procedure and not a repair.

An agent asked to talk a customer through "cleaning the drain filter" on a WWB 020
is being asked for a procedure this manual does not contain. The correct answer is
to say so and offer the supported path, not to adapt another model's steps.

---

## 3. Reset

No customer reset sequence is documented for this model.

---

## 4. Technical Service

The manual carries a **"Technical Service"** section and a **"Contact in case of
fault"** section. Where the problem-solving guide does not resolve the symptom, the
documented path is Miele Technical Service — replacement parts are obtained from
"Miele's customer service, or your Miele dealer."

---

## 5. Safety — stop use

Stop use immediately and arrange service, regardless of what the control field
shows, on: smoke, a burning or chemical smell, sparks, a hot cabinet, or water
pooling under the machine.
