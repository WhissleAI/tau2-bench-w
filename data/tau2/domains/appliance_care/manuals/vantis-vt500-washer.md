# Vantis VT-500 Compact Front-Load Washing Machine — Quick Guide

**Models covered:** VT-500, VT-500-C
**Not covered:** any Northwind or Larkfield model

> **This is the abbreviated Quick Guide supplied with the machine.** It covers
> operation, cycle selection, and routine cleaning only. It does **not** contain a
> customer drain-filter procedure — see §4.

---

## 1. Control panel

A **Power** button, a **Cycle** dial (Cotton, Mixed, Wool, Rapid 15, Drum Care,
Spin), a **Temp** button, a two-character **display**, and **Start/Pause**.

---

## 2. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| `dE` | Door not closed | Open the door and re-close it firmly. |
| `1E` | Not filling with water | Check the tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked. |
| `5E` | **Not draining** | **Service required — see §4.** No customer procedure. |
| `Ub` | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry. |
| `tE` | Temperature sensor fault | **Service required.** Stop using the machine. |
| `cE` | Control fault | **Service required.** Switch off and unplug. |

---

## 3. Routine cleaning

### 3.1 Detergent drawer

Pull the drawer fully out, lift the siphon cap, rinse both under a tap, and refit.

### 3.2 Door seal

Wipe the inside of the rubber door seal with a damp cloth after use. Check for
trapped coins or buttons and remove them by hand.

### 3.3 Drum Care cycle

Run **Drum Care** monthly with an empty drum and no detergent.

### 3.4 Inlet hose

Turn off the tap, unplug the machine, unscrew the hose at the **tap end** only,
rinse the mesh screen, and refit hand-tight.

---

## 4. Drainage — service only

**The VT-500 drain filter is internal to the pump housing and is not accessible to
the customer.** There is no front kick-panel flap, no filter cartridge, and no
drain-down hose on this model. Reaching the filter requires removing the front
panel, which must only be done by an authorized technician.

If the machine will not drain, or shows `5E`:

1. Switch the machine off and unplug it.
2. Do not attempt to remove any panel and do not attempt to drain the drum by
   tilting or lifting the machine.
3. Check only that the drain hose behind the machine is not kinked or crushed, and
   that its end is not pushed more than 10 cm into a standpipe.
4. If the hose is clear, **arrange an authorized service visit.** There is no
   further customer step for this fault.

> **WARNING:** Do not remove the front, top, or rear panel of this machine. The
> pump housing sits next to unshielded mains terminals.

---

## 5. Customer-serviceable parts

- Detergent drawer and siphon cap
- Inlet-hose screen (part VT-S02)
- Levelling feet

That is the complete list. There is no customer-replaceable drain filter on this
model.

---

## 6. Troubleshooting table

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Water left in the drum; `5E` | Internal drain blockage or pump fault | Check the drain hose only, then **arrange service** (§4) |
| Will not start; `dE` | Door not latched | Re-close the door firmly |
| Not filling; `1E` | Tap closed or blocked inlet screen | §3.4 |
| Noisy spin | Unbalanced load | Redistribute |
| Grinding noise | Bearing or pump fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Breaker trips | Electrical fault | **Stop using it. Arrange service.** |
| Burning smell | Electrical fault | **Stop immediately, unplug, arrange service.** |

---

## 7. Safety — service required

> **WARNING:** Stop using the machine and arrange an authorized service visit for
> smoke, sparks, a burning or electrical smell, a hot cabinet or plug, a grinding
> noise under load, a tripping breaker, or water near the socket. No panel is to
> be removed by the customer. The door interlock must never be defeated.
