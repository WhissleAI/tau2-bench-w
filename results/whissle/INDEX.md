## How to read a row

- **N** is the scored denominator, never the attempted count. **Excl.** is what was thrown away and why it matters — a headline over a heavily excluded run is a statement about the survivors.
- **Judge** answers one question: was the grader independent of the agent's vendor? `no` means the number is a regression instrument, not a leaderboard result. `n/a` means grading was deterministic and the question does not arise.
- **Prelim.** marks a run below the sample-size threshold, or one whose directory was incomplete when the report was generated.
