**Clinical Summary:**
Your baby's symptoms are consistent with Hirschsprung's disease, a condition where nerve cells (ganglion cells) are missing in a segment of the colon. This prevents normal bowel muscle contractions, leading to obstruction. This is a surgical condition requiring prompt referral to pediatric surgery.

**Immediate recommendations:**
- NPO (nothing by mouth) status
- Nasogastric tube placement for decompression
- IV fluid resuscitation
- Urgent pediatric surgical consultation
- Possible emergency colostomy or primary repair depending on the extent of aganglionosis
