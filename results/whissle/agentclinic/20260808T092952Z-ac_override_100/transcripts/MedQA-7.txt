DIAGNOSIS READY: Congenital Rubella Syndrome

Your baby's symptoms—the heart murmur, the bluish spots that don't blanch, the cloudiness in both eyes, and the hearing loss—are all classic features of this condition, which occurs when rubella infection crosses the placenta during early pregnancy. This is why comprehensive prenatal screening and rubella vaccination before pregnancy are so important. Your baby will need ongoing specialist care including cardiology, ophthalmology, and audiology to manage these complications.
