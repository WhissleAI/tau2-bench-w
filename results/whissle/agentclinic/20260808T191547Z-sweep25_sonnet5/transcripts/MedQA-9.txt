PATIENT: Yes, the pain is definitely in my lower abdomen, and it really hurts when that area is pressed or touched. I haven't noticed any foul-smelling discharge though - everything seems normal in that regard.
DOCTOR: Have you had any chills, increased heart rate, or noticed heavy or unusual vaginal bleeding since delivery?
PATIENT: Yes, I've had some chills along with the fever - they come and go. I haven't really noticed my heart racing or anything like that, and the bleeding seems pretty normal for after delivery, not unusually heavy.
