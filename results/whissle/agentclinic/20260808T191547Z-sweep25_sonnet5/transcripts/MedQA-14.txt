# MedQA-14  (MedQA, text mode)
correct diagnosis: Rupture of the flexor digitorum profundus tendon at its point of insertion
outcome: correct

DOCTOR: How long has your right ring finger been swollen and painful, and did it start after any injury, cut, or splinter?
PATIENT: It started about 2 days ago when I was playing football. My finger got caught in another player's jersey and he pulled away really hard. It's been swollen and painful ever since.
DOCTOR: Was there any visible deformity, or could you bend and straighten the finger normally right after the injury?
PATIENT: Right after it happened, I could still move it a little, but now I can't bend the tip of my finger at all when I try to make a fist. The finger also kind of stays extended even when I'm not trying to move it.
DOCTOR: Doctor: That sounds like a "jersey finger" — is the pain and swelling mostly around the last joint near the fingertip, and is there any bruising there?
PATIENT: Yes, the pain and swelling are definitely worst right at that last joint near the tip. There is some bruising there too, and it's pretty tender when I touch it.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Can you feel any tenderness or a lump in your palm near the base of that finger, where the tendon may have retracted?
PATIENT: I'm not really sure, let me check... No, I don't feel any obvious lump in my palm. The pain and tenderness are really just at the fingertip joint itself.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Understood. I'd like to get an X-ray of the finger to rule out an avulsion fracture at the site of tendon attachment.

REQUEST TEST: Hand_XRay_Right_Ring_Finger
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS: X-ray of the Right Hand shows no bone fractures or dislocations visible. Soft tissue swelling noted around the distal interphalangeal joint of the right ring finger.
DOCTOR: Doctor: No fracture, which fits a soft-tissue jersey finger — a flexor digitorum profundus tendon avulsion from the bone. This needs prompt hand-surgeon evaluation since delayed repair worsens outcomes.

DIAGNOSIS READY: Jersey finger (Flexor Digitorum Profundus tendon avulsion) of the right ring finger, Leddy-Packer type likely I-II given no bony fragment on X-ray — refer urgently to hand surgery.
