DOCTOR: Dr. Agent: Hello, I'm Dr. Agent — could you tell me about the newborn's specific concerns? What symptoms or findings prompted this visit?
PATIENT: Well, doctor, I'm the mother. The nurses mentioned they heard some kind of heart murmur when they examined my baby, and she also didn't pass the hearing test they did. I've also noticed some bluish spots on her skin that have been there since birth.
DOCTOR: Dr. Agent: I see — a heart murmur, failed hearing screen, and bluish skin spots since birth. Can you tell me more about these skin spots — are they flat, discolored patches (like slate-gray/bluish birthmarks), or do they look more like the baby's skin turning blue (cyanosis) especially with crying or feeding?
PATIENT: They're flat, bluish-gray patches, not like her skin is turning blue or anything. They don't go away when I press on them, and they've just been there since she was born.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Did you have any illnesses, rash, or fever during your pregnancy, or was your prenatal care and vaccination history (like rubella) up to date?
PATIENT: I didn't get any prenatal care during my pregnancy, so I don't really know about my vaccination history. I don't remember having any rash or fever while I was pregnant though.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Has anyone noticed any eye problems in the baby — like clouding of the eyes (cataracts), unusual eye movements, or poor visual tracking? Also, was she born small for her gestational age?
PATIENT: Actually, now that you mention it, one of the nurses did say something about her eyes looking a bit cloudy when they examined her. I'm not sure what that means exactly. As for her size, they told me she was about 6 and a half pounds, which I think is pretty normal for a full-term baby.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Given the murmur, sensorineural hearing loss, and cloudy eyes (possible cataracts) along with lack of prenatal care, I'd like to check for congenital rubella syndrome. REQUEST TEST: Echocardiogram, and REQUEST TEST: TORCH_titers (including rubella IgM).
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS: 

**Echocardiogram:**
- Patent ductus arteriosus (PDA) identified

**TORCH Screen:**
- Rubella IgM: **Positive**
- Other TORCH pathogens (Toxoplasmosis, CMV, HSV): Negative

**Additional findings from examinations:**
- Ophthalmologic evaluation confirms: Congenital cataracts (bilateral)
- Hearing test confirms: Bilateral sensorineural hearing loss
- Cardiac examination: Continuous 'machinery' murmur consistent with PDA

These results, combined with the clinical presentation of bluish macules (blueberry muffin spots), support the diagnosis of **Congenital Rubella Syndrome**.
DOCTOR: DIAGNOSIS READY: Congenital Rubella Syndrome — confirmed by positive rubella IgM, PDA, bilateral sensorineural hearing loss, bilateral congenital cataracts, and "blueberry muffin" dermal erythropoiesis spots, in the setting of no prenatal care/immunization.
