DOCTOR: Doctor: Have you noticed any other symptoms along with the unsteadiness—such as slurred speech, double vision, weakness, numbness, or memory problems?
PATIENT: Yes, actually, I have noticed some weakness in my right arm. It's been harder to grip things and lift objects like I normally would. But I haven't had any slurred speech or vision problems that I've noticed.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Have you had any significant medical history—such as cancer, alcohol use disorder, autoimmune disease, or recent infections—that you think might be relevant?
PATIENT: Well, I do have Crohn disease, which was diagnosed about two years ago. It's been pretty severe and hasn't responded well to the usual treatments, so my gastroenterologist started me on natalizumab about a year ago.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Thank you, that's very helpful. Have you noticed any changes in your vision, cognition, or personality—like trouble concentrating, confusion, or difficulty finding words—since these symptoms started?
PATIENT: No, I haven't really noticed anything like that. My thinking seems pretty clear, and I haven't had trouble with my words or concentration. It's really just the walking and the weakness in my right arm that's been bothering me.
DOCTOR: Doctor: Given your natalizumab use, I'd like to get an MRI of your brain to look for any changes suggestive of PML. REQUEST TEST: MRI_Brain_with_contrast
