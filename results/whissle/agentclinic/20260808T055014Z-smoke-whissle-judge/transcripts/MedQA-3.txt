DOCTOR: DIAGNOSIS READY: Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)

The constellation of findings—massive lymphadenopathy across multiple sites, splenomegaly, B symptoms (night sweats, weight loss, fatigue), elevated LDH, and most critically the lymph node biopsy showing CD20-positive B lymphocytes with high proliferative index—is classic for DLBCL, an aggressive non-Hodgkin lymphoma. You'll need immediate referral to oncology for staging, treatment planning, and likely chemotherapy, but we've identified this early enough to begin care promptly.
