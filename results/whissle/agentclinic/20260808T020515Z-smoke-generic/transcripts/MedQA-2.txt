**Clinical Context:**
The fact that your baby passed meconium normally after birth but is now presenting at 8 months with progressive symptoms is classic for short-segment disease, where initial compensation gradually fails as the child grows and functional demands increase.

While a rectal biopsy would provide histological confirmation (absence of ganglion cells in the aganglionic segment), the clinical and radiological evidence is sufficiently diagnostic for **surgical consultation and intervention planning**. This typically involves a pull-through procedure to connect normally innervated bowel to the rectum.

Immediate pediatric surgical referral is recommended.
