DOCTOR: Hello, I'm Dr. Agent. Let's take a look at that chest skin lesion—how long has it been there?
PATIENT: I first noticed it about 2 months ago. I think it's gotten a bit bigger since then, but I'm not entirely sure.
DOCTOR: Has the lesion changed in color or started bleeding at all?
PATIENT: No, it hasn't bled or anything like that. I can't say I've really noticed much change in the color either - it just seems to have gotten larger is all.
DOCTOR: Has the lesion caused you any discomfort, such as itching or pain?
PATIENT: No, it doesn't hurt at all and it's not itchy either. It's just kind of there, which is why I almost didn't come in about it.
DOCTOR: Does the lesion have irregular borders, or appear to have multiple different colors within it?
PATIENT: Hmm, I'm not really sure about irregular borders - I haven't looked at it that closely. It doesn't seem to have multiple colors though, it looks pretty much the same color throughout.
DOCTOR: REQUEST TEST: Dermoscopy
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS: Based on the physical examination findings available, the dermoscopy would reveal:

**Dermoscopy Findings:**
- **Asymmetry**: Present - the lesion shows asymmetrical features
- **Border**: Irregular borders noted
- **Color**: Varied coloration observed throughout the lesion
- **Diameter**: Greater than 6mm
- **Evolution**: Lesion has increased in size over 2-month period

**Additional Features:**
- Partly elevated lesion
- Firm consistency on palpation
- Located on chest

**Clinical Significance**: These dermoscopic features are concerning and meet multiple criteria of the ABCDE rule for melanoma (Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variation, Diameter >6mm, Evolution/change over time). Further evaluation with skin biopsy is recommended.
DOCTOR: REQUEST TEST: Skin_Biopsy
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS: 

**Skin Biopsy - Histopathology Report**

**Specimen:** Skin lesion from chest

**Histopathology Findings:** 
Atypical melanocytes that infiltrate the epidermis and dermis, confirming the diagnosis of malignant melanoma.

**Interpretation:** 
The histopathological examination reveals the presence of atypical melanocytic cells with infiltration through both the epidermal and dermal layers of the skin. This finding is diagnostic of malignant melanoma.

**Clinical Correlation:**
This biopsy result confirms malignant melanoma, consistent with the clinical presentation of an asymmetric, irregularly bordered lesion with color variation and diameter >6mm that has demonstrated growth over a 2-month period.

**Recommendation:**
Immediate referral to oncology/surgical oncology for staging and treatment planning is indicated.
DOCTOR: DIAGNOSIS READY: Malignant melanoma
