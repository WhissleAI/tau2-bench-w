DOCTOR: Hello, I'm Dr. Agent. Can you tell me exactly when this lower back pain started and if you experienced any injury or heavy lifting?
PATIENT: It started a few hours ago while I was at the gym doing deadlifts. I didn't feel like I lifted anything unusually heavy - it was my normal weight - but suddenly I felt this sharp pain shoot through my lower back.
DOCTOR: Did the sharp pain shoot down past your knee or into your foot when it happened?
PATIENT: Yes, it shot all the way down the side of my leg and into my foot - it was really intense and sudden. The pain in my foot is still there, especially when I try to move or bend.
DOCTOR: Have you noticed any numbness, tingling, or weakness in that same leg or foot since the pain started?
PATIENT: Yes, actually there is some tingling in my foot, especially on the top and outer side. I haven't noticed any major weakness, but the tingling is pretty uncomfortable and constant.
DOCTOR: Have you experienced any changes in your bowel or bladder control since the pain began?
PATIENT: No, nothing like that - my bowel and bladder function have been completely normal. It's really just the back pain and the tingling in my leg and foot that's bothering me.

**Neurological Examination:**

- **Sensation:** Intact to light touch and pin-prick throughout the leg and foot
- **Motor Function:** No significant weakness noted
