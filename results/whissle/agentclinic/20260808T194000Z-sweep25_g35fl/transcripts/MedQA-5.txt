DOCTOR: REQUEST TEST: Knee_MRI
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS: MRI of the knee can be considered if clinical suspicion persists despite negative initial workup. However, based on your clinical presentation - recent increase in volleyball activity, pain localized to the medial aspect of the tibia just below the patella, pain with resisted knee extension, negative X-ray, and physical exam findings of tenderness over the tibial tubercle area without ligamentous instability - the diagnosis can be made clinically without mandatory MRI at this time.

If MRI were to be performed, it would likely show inflammation and edema at the patellar tendon insertion site on the tibial tubercle, consistent with patellar tendinopathy or apophysitis.
DOCTOR: DIAGNOSIS READY: Patellar tendinopathy
