The imaging pattern you're showing—a smooth, polylobulated mass on mammogram with a normal ultrasound, combined with rapid growth but no systemic symptoms—is most consistent with a benign phyllodes tumor, which is a common breast fibroepithelial tumor that can grow quickly but remains non-cancerous in most cases.
