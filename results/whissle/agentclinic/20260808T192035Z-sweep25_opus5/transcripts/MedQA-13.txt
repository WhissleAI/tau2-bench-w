# MedQA-13  (MedQA, text mode)
correct diagnosis: Hirschsprung’s disease
outcome: correct

DOCTOR: Hello, I'm Dr. Agent — I understand your baby has been vomiting green fluid and hasn't passed stool. How old is your baby, and when did the vomiting start?
PATIENT: My baby is 2 days old, and the vomiting started yesterday. The fluid coming up has been green, and it's really worrying me because he still hasn't had a bowel movement since he was born.
DOCTOR: This is urgent — bilious vomiting in a newborn is a surgical emergency, so he needs to be seen right away while we work this up. Was the pregnancy complicated by excess amniotic fluid (polyhydramnios), and is there any family history of cystic fibrosis or bowel problems?
PATIENT: No, the pregnancy was normal as far as I know — I didn't have any issues with too much fluid or anything like that. And there's no family history of cystic fibrosis or bowel problems that I'm aware of.
DOCTOR: Thank you — his belly looks distended, so let me get imaging right away to see where the blockage is.

REQUEST TEST: Abdominal_X-Ray (supine and upright) and Upper_GI_Contrast_Study
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS:

**Abdominal X-ray:**
- Dilated loops of bowel with no gas in the rectum, suggestive of a distal obstruction.

**Upper GI Contrast Study:**
NORMAL READINGS
DOCTOR: The upper GI is normal, so the blockage is lower down — has anyone examined his bottom to confirm he has a normal anal opening, and did any stool or gas come out after a rectal exam?
PATIENT: I don't think anyone has done that kind of exam yet. He definitely has an opening there from what I can see, but no stool or gas has come out at all since he was born.
DOCTOR: Was your pregnancy complicated by diabetes, or were you given magnesium sulfate during labor?

REQUEST TEST: Contrast_Enema (water-soluble) and Rectal_Suction_Biopsy
MEASUREMENT: RESULTS:

**Contrast Enema (water-soluble):**
NORMAL READINGS

**Rectal Suction Biopsy:**
- **Histopathology:** Absence of ganglion cells in the submucosa and myenteric plexus, consistent with Hirschsprung's disease.
DOCTOR: The biopsy shows absent ganglion cells in his bowel wall, which explains why nothing is passing through — this is Hirschsprung's disease, and pediatric surgery needs to see him now.

DIAGNOSIS READY: Hirschsprung's disease (congenital aganglionic megacolon)
